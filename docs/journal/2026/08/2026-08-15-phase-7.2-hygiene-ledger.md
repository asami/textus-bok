## HYG-P7.2-001: Public Selector Nominal-Wrapper Policy

Status: OPEN

Discovery state and date: Phase 7.2 REVIEW, 2026-08-15.

Repository and location: `textus-bok`,
`src/main/cozy/textus-bok.cml`, `BokProfile` and `BokProjectId`.

Evidence: normal CAR lint reports `cml.datatype.nominal-string-wrapper` for the
two new string-backed selectors. Generated request validation executes before
`BokProfileSelection.normalize`; adding scalar constraints solely to silence
the warning could replace the stable `invalid-selection` and
`project-identity-required` failures with generic request-validation failures.
No supported inline CML suppression was found.

Category and risk: tooling-policy hygiene, low runtime risk. The generated
schema expresses the selector fields, while normalization, authorization, and
structured failure behavior are covered by the Phase 7.2 executable
specifications and the 51-test full suite.

Outside-phase reason: changing scalar validation policy is not required for
the exact read-selection behavior and could alter the closed public failure
contract. The existing nominal wrappers remain owned by `P7-HY-03`; this item
records only the two Phase 7.2 additions.

Follow-up boundary: add an explicit Cozy/CAR-lint policy for resolver-validated
nominal selectors, or design a compatible constrained-value contract with its
own migration and failure-boundary evidence. Do not weaken Phase 7.2 failures
merely to clear the lint warning.
